Craft Experiences

Participatory Making

Workshop

Block Printing Workshop

Collaborative textile printing where visitors self-print, experience the pressure of the block, and add to a shared textile surface.

Live Making

Pottery

Live clay making shaped as storytelling through craft, earth, hand, form, and inherited technique.

Script

Calligraphy

Indian language exploration with visitor participation through writing, stencils, and guided mark-making.

Textile

Saree Draping

Contemporary draping exploration that treats six yards of fabric as design, identity, sculpture, and personal movement.

Sustainable Styling

Upcycled Bandana

A sustainable styling experience using textile reuse for fashion experimentation and personal expression.

Wall Installation

Rangoli Wall

A collective wall installation where each visitor contributes through sticker placement.

Adornment

Bindi Experience

A temporary adornment station exploring placement, identity, intimacy, and cultural symbolism.

Material and Cultural Explorations

Archives of Matter

Material Culture

Spice Archive

Spices are presented through agricultural heritage, colour, texture, and global trade significance.

Natural Perfumery

Fragrance Chamber

Material ingredients, craftsmanship, memory, and cultural storytelling through fragrance history.

Botanical Knowledge

Ayurvedic Products

Beauty and wellness traditions presented through ingredient exploration and material heritage.

Interactive Sound

Musical Instruments

Craftsmanship of instrument-making, resonance, tactile rhythm exploration, performance traditions, and visitor interaction.

Immersive Installation

The Womb
of OM

An immersive tunnel centred on origin, vibration, sensory transition, and spatial philosophy. The installation is designed as an architecture-inspired threshold where sound, light, fabric, and enclosure create a moment of reflection.

Visitors enter a slower spatial rhythm before returning to the surrounding exhibition with heightened attention to material and memory.

Bindi to Brera

Bindi to Brera is a contemporary Indian cultural exhibition in Milan exploring craft, sensory memory, material intelligence, and participatory design.
