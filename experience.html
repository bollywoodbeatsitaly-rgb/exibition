The Emotional Framework

Nine
Rasas

The exhibition is structured around the nine Rasas — Sanskrit emotional essences from Indian aesthetic philosophy. Every stall, workshop, and installation maps to one or more of these states.

What Is a Rasa

Feeling
as
Framework

Rasa — Sanskrit for "essence" or "flavour" — is a 2,000-year-old Indian aesthetic concept describing the emotional states that art and experience can evoke. The nine Rasas form the basis of classical Indian performing arts, poetry, and design.

Bindi to Brera uses this framework to organise the exhibition. Every encounter — from block printing to the Womb of OM — is intentionally placed within an emotional register. The result is not a cultural display but an emotional journey through Indian aesthetic philosophy.

The Nine Emotional States

The Rasas

Srngara

Beauty

The rasa of adornment, intimacy, and sensory pleasure. The world seen through the lens of beauty and desire.

→ Indian Jewellery · Bindi & Tattoo · Saree Draping · Fragrance Chamber

Hasya

Play & Joy

The rasa of laughter, lightness, and playful creation — when making mistakes becomes part of the joy.

→ Block Printing · Upcycled Bandana

Karuna

Softness & Care

The rasa of compassion and tenderness — the therapeutic process of making, healing, and nurturing.

→ Ayurvedic Products · Live Pottery

Raudra

Intensity & Heat

The rasa of force, passion, and raw energy — the overwhelming heat and power of India's material world.

→ Spice Archive

Vira

Energy & Mastery

The rasa of skill, confidence, and disciplined action — craft performed with precision and transformative power.

→ Block Printing · Saree Draping · Calligraphy

Bhayanka

Awe & the Unknown

The rasa of overwhelm and mystery — the confrontation with something larger than the self.

→ The Womb of OM

Adhbutha

Wonder & Discovery

The rasa of astonishment — when a pattern emerges, a language transforms visually, a sound is felt physically.

→ Musical Instruments · Block Printing · Calligraphy

Bibhatsa

Raw Material Truth

The rasa of the unprocessed and unrefined — matter in its origin state before it becomes commodity or luxury.

→ Spice Archive · Live Pottery · Ayurvedic Products

Santa

Calm & Balance

The rasa of peace and equanimity — rest, sensory reset, and the quiet that follows full engagement.

→ Ayurvedic Products
