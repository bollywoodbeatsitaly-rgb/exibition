Multi-Sensory Journey

FIVE
SENSES

The exhibition is structured into five experiential zones, each designed around a core sense — together creating a layered narrative of India that moves beyond observation into full sensory immersion and participation.

🤲   Zone 01
Material & Craft

TOUCH

This zone focuses on India's rich craft heritage through tactile interaction. Visitors engage directly with textiles, handmade materials, and artisan processes — experiencing the depth of Indian craftsmanship through touch and making.

From the delicate embroidery of Chanakya International's swatch books to the raw leather of Kolhapuri chappals, from hand-woven rugs to block-printed fabrics — every surface tells a story of generations of mastery.

🧵

Chanakya International — Swatch Book Exhibition

Explore exquisite textile swatches wearing gloves — a museum-like interaction

 👡

Kolhapuri Chappals

Indian & Italian leather craftsmanship in dialogue

 🪆

Rugs as Art

Indian weaving traditions displayed as immersive craftsmanship

 🎨

Block Printing Workshop

Feel the "thump" — a community textile built stamp by stamp

 🪡

Upcycled Bandana Shop

Sustainable fashion from recycled saree fabrics
👃   Zone 02
Aroma & Memory

SMELL

A sensory exploration of fragrance as cultural memory. From Ayurvedic ingredients to spices, incense, and traditional perfumes, this zone captures the emotional and ritualistic role of scent in everyday Indian life.

These fragrances are not presented merely as products, but as emotional markers deeply tied to rituals, comfort, spirituality, and memory.

🌶️

The Masala Story

Spices from across India — see, touch, and smell centuries of culinary heritage

 🏺

Itra Stall — Scent Library

Oud, Sandalwood, Rooh Gulab — the perfumes of Mughal courts

 🕯️

Dhoop Bathi — Incense

Sustainable Indian incense brands and their spiritual heritage

 🌿

Mouth Freshener Stall

Sweet blends and spiced masala mixes — essential Indian food culture
👁   Zone 03
Visual Culture & Identity

SIGHT

A visual journey through India's artistic and cultural expressions. This zone includes cinema, traditional crafts, jewellery, installations, and participatory art — reflecting the diversity and vibrancy of Indian visual identity.

From the iconic films that shaped Indian society to the rangoli art that adorns every doorstep — sight is how India communicates beauty, devotion, and celebration.

🎬

Movie Room

Iconic Indian films — mirrors of everyday Indian life

 🌸

Rangoli Wall — You Complete It

A giant half-finished wall built by hundreds of hands

 🪔

Pottery Live Demo

Watch the birth of a story in clay — from kulhad to matka

 ✍️

Calligraphy & Languages

Indian scripts and typography — write your name in ancient art

 💎

Indian Jewellery

Jhumkas, bangles, anklets — a vibrant festive experience
🍵   Zone 04
Street & Ritual Consumption

TASTE

This zone introduces India through flavour and hospitality. Visitors experience traditional tastes such as chai, spices, and mouth fresheners — highlighting food as a cultural connector and everyday ritual.

From the legendary South Indian filter coffee poured tumbler to davara, to the communal summer ritual of sun-drying papad — taste is how India says welcome.

☕

Chai Tea Stall

Free sachets — the conversation starter of a billion people

 🫖

Filter Coffee

South Indian ritual — tumbler to davara, a performance of culture

 🫓

Papad Stall

"Grandmother's Geometry" — ochre, turmeric, slow-made

 🌶️

Food Court — Taste of India

North, South, East, West — all of India's regional cuisines
✋   Zone 05
Participation & Creation

ACTION

A fully interactive zone where visitors become co-creators. Through workshops, games, personalization, and collective installations, visitors actively contribute to the exhibition and build their own cultural memory.

Whether it's draping a saree, making a puppet from fabric scraps, or playing Gilli Danda — the Action zone is where India comes alive through your hands.

🪆

 ✨

 🏏

India's oldest street game — pure joy, pure India

 🌿

Ayurvedic Products

Amla, neem, hibiscus — India's natural beauty heritage
