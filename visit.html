The Passport Program

YOUR PERSONAL
STORY OF
INDIA

Every visitor gets a blank postcard at the entrance — and that is just the beginning. As they move through each stop, they collect a unique stamp in a traditional Indian design.

By the time they reach the exit, their postcard is no longer blank — it is a beautiful piece of Indian art, built stamp by stamp, stop by stop. Something they made without even realising it.

Not just a souvenir — it is your personal story of India.

Frequently Asked Questions

GOOD
TO KNOW

Is entry really free?

Yes — Intreccio Rasa is completely free and open to the public. No tickets, no registration required. Simply arrive and begin your journey.

Where exactly is the venue?

The event will be held at either Fabbrica del Vapore or Parco Sempione in Milan, subject to Comune di Milano availability. Exact venue will be confirmed closer to the date.

Can I bring children?

Absolutely — Intreccio Rasa is family-friendly. There is a dedicated Kids Corner with puppet shows, face painting, Indian storytelling, and the Gilli Danda corner especially for younger visitors.

Are the workshops bookable?

Most workshops are walk-in on the day. Some sessions (like Saree Draping and Jaipur Puppet) may have limited capacity — arrive early to secure your spot.

Is food available at the event?

Yes — the Food Court offers regional Indian cuisine from North, South, East, and West India. The Chai Tea Stall offers free tea samples. All food operators hold valid permits and use eco-friendly packaging.

How long should I plan to stay?

We recommend 3–4 hours to experience all five zones, watch at least one performance, and participate in a workshop. The full day (10:00–20:00) allows you to see everything at a relaxed pace.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes — accessibility is a priority. Both proposed venues (Fabbrica del Vapore and Parco Sempione) are accessible to visitors with reduced mobility. Detailed accessibility information will be provided upon venue confirmation.

Can I buy items at the stalls?

Many stalls will have items available for purchase — including Indian jewellery, Kolhapuri chappals, Ayurvedic products, Itra perfumes, and the upcycled bandana collection. Bring cash as well as cards.
