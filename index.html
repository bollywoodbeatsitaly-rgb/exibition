Our Story →
"Born from the fusion of intreccio — Italian for intertwining — and rasa — Sanskrit for emotion, essence, and aesthetic experience."

Interrasa is not an exhibition you observe. It is a world you enter, feel, and move through. The storyline follows the rhythm of an ordinary Indian day, transforming familiar moments into immersive cultural experiences.

Five experiential zones, each designed around a core sense. A live dialogue between Indian culture and Milan's design community — open to all, free entry.

✦ Cultural Exhibition · Art · Dance · Craft · Heritage · Milan 2026
