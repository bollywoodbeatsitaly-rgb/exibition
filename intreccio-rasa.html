About The Event

WHERE
CULTURE
BECOMES
EMOTION

"Born from the fusion of intreccio — Italian for intertwining — and rasa — Sanskrit for emotion, essence, and aesthetic experience."

Interrasa is not an exhibition you observe. It is a world you enter, feel, and move through. The storyline follows the rhythm of an ordinary Indian day, transforming familiar moments into immersive cultural experiences.

As visitors move through the space, they step into fragments of daily life that together weave a larger narrative of identity, craftsmanship, memory, and connection. Five experiential zones, each designed around a core sense.

The event is a public exhibition — open to all. Milanese citizens, the Italian public, and the 50,000+ Indian diaspora in Lombardy. A live dialogue between Indian culture and Milan's design community.

THE
STALLS

Over 25 immersive experiences, workshops, and installations celebrating the authentic beauty of Indian everyday life in Milan.

Touch · Zone 01

Chanakya International — Swatch Book Exhibition

A curated swatch book exhibition showcasing textiles and fabric samples. Guests explore swatches wearing gloves — a premium, museum-like interaction emphasising the value and delicacy of Indian craftsmanship.

Touch · Zone 01

Kolhapuri Chappals

A dialogue between Indian and Italian leather craftsmanship — celebrating shared appreciation for quality, heritage, and timeless artisanship across cultures.

Touch · Zone 01

Rugs as Art

Indian rugs as immersive pieces of craftsmanship. Weaves, patterns, and the musical traditions woven into sitting on the floor — humility, togetherness, respect.

🪡
Touch · Zone 01

Upcycled Bandana Shop

Sustainable fashion using recycled saree fabrics. Style them as bandanas, scarves, or wrist ties — creativity meets Indian textile heritage.

🎨
Touch · Zone 01

Block Printing Workshop

The "thump" — the physical force to transfer pigment. A community textile built stamp by stamp by every visitor throughout the day.

Smell · Zone 02

The Masala Story

Stopped not by a banner — but by the smell of freshly brewed chai and the rich aroma of Indian spices. A masala table loaded with spices from across India. Two senses, one stall, the whole story of India in a single stop.

Smell · Zone 02

Itra Stall — Scent Library

Transporting guests to 17th-century Mughal courts. Explore Oud, Sandalwood, and the legendary Rooh Gulab — the "slow perfume" that melts into the skin.

🕯️
Smell · Zone 02

Dhoop Bathi — Incense

A collaborative exhibit with sustainable Indian incense brands. Fragrance as ritual, spirituality, meditation, and everyday Indian life woven through generations.

🌿
Smell · Zone 02

Mouth Freshener Stall

Sweet blends to spiced masala mixes — an essential part of Indian food culture. Visitors sample traditional mouth fresheners as Indians do on every street and market.

Sight · Zone 03

Movie Room

Iconic Indian films — cinematic milestones and mirrors of everyday Indian life. Storytelling traditions and visual language that shaped the country's identity globally.

Sight · Zone 03

Rangoli Wall — You Complete It

A giant half-finished Rangoli wall. Every visitor adds their sticker — by the end of the exhibition the wall becomes a masterpiece made by hundreds of hands together. Participation as art form.

Sight · Zone 03

Pottery Live Demo

The artisan sells the "birth" of a story. Every brushstroke bridges the soil of India and the eyes of Milan. From tiny kulhads to massive matkas.

✍️
Sight · Zone 03

Calligraphy & Languages

A creative space celebrating Indian scripts and typography. Write your name using stencils or with a professional calligrapher. The beauty of Indian handwritten art forms.

💎
Sight · Zone 03

Indian Jewellery

A curated collection of jhumkas, bangles, anklets, nose rings, and layered necklaces. Craftsmanship, colors, and ornamentation culture from across India's regions.

Taste · Zone 04

Chai Tea Stall

India has one of the richest tea cultures in the world. Free sachets of tea to taste and take home. A warm cup — the conversation starter of a billion people. We turn foot traffic into genuine engagement.

Taste · Zone 04

Filter Coffee

The legendary South Indian filter coffee — poured from a steel tumbler to a davara. Not just a drink: a ritual, a tradition, a piece of culture that millions wake up to every morning.

Taste · Zone 04

Papad Stall

"Grandmother's Geometry" — the precision of sun-dried lentil crackers. Ochre, turmeric yellow, terracotta: organic, artisanal, and gloriously slow-made.

🌶️
Taste · Zone 04

Food Court — Taste of India

Street-food style area with regional stalls covering North, South, East, and West Indian cuisines. Masala Chai corner and traditional Indian sweets. All eco-friendly packaging.

Action · Zone 05

Jaipur Puppet Workshop — The Storytellers

Using leftover katrans (fabric scraps) as the base for doll clothing. Personalize the faces, add detailing of your choice — take home a meaningful souvenir. Sustainability and mindful reuse in every stitch.

Action · Zone 05

Bindi & Temporary Tattoo

Interactive beauty station inspired by Indian festive makeup. Hand-painted motifs inspired by henna and traditional art. Playful self-expression and cultural exploration.

Action · Zone 05

Saree Draping Station

Six yards of elegance. Learn the art of the drape — a garment with no zippers or buttons, only the grace of the person wearing it. Photo corner included.

🪃
Action · Zone 05

Gilli Danda Corner

One of India's oldest street games. Kids put down their phones and pick up a danda — while parents enjoy chai next door. Pure joy, pure India.

🌺
Action · Zone 05

Ayurvedic Products

Traditional Indian beauty rituals and Ayurvedic self-care. Explore amla, neem, hibiscus, sandalwood, and turmeric. Wellness, sustainability, and India's heritage in natural beauty care.

Special Installation · The Neon Betel

PAANWADI
AESTHETIC

Stepping into a technicolor time capsule where the street corner meets the gallery. An immersive installation reimagining the iconic Indian Paan Wadi through the lens of Indian Kitsch and Maximalism.

In the heart of India, the Paan stall is more than a shop — it is a social hub, a burst of flavor, and a visual explosion of organized chaos. We are bringing this to Milan.

The Passport Program

YOUR PERSONAL
STORY OF INDIA

Every visitor gets a blank postcard at the entrance — and that is just the beginning. As they move through each stop, they collect a unique stamp in a traditional Indian design. By the time they reach the exit, their postcard is no longer blank — it is a beautiful piece of Indian art, built stamp by stamp, stop by stop. Something they made without even realising it.

✦ Not just a souvenir — it is their personal story of India ✦
